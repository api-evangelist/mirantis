MOSK 26.1 strengthens lifecycle management, OpenStack networking, and access to operational knowledge. For infrastructure operators, that means better backup and upgrade workflows, more flexibility in how clouds are configured and managed over time, and faster access to guidance when working across a complex stack of components and services. For cloud users, it means stronger networking capabilities for demanding use cases, more predictable performance, and a platform they can rely on.
