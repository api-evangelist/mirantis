As AI adoption accelerates, enterprises must align infrastructure choices with both technical and regulatory realities. The AI infrastructure stack consists of the foundational technologies that support AI workloads below the application layer. Understanding its core components, how the layers connect, and how to optimize them is essential for building scalable and future-ready enterprise AI infrastructure.
